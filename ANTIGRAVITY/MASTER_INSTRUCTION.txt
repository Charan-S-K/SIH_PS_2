## 8. ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

The product is an evidence-first passive email cryptographic forensics platform.

Architecture principle:

`Facts → Rules → Evidence → ML → Prioritization → Recommendation`

Do NOT build:

`PCAP → LLM → arbitrary verdict`

Deterministic protocol/TLS/certificate facts must remain authoritative.

ML should assist with:
- risk classification;
- anomaly detection;
- prioritization;
- explainability.

The system must explicitly support:

`UNKNOWN / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`

when passive PCAP data does not contain enough evidence.

Never invent certificate, TLS, cipher, packet, or session facts.

Preserve evidence references such as:
- packet/frame numbers;
- timestamps;
- TCP stream/session IDs;
- protocol;
- handshake information;
- certificate details;
- relevant packet ranges.

## 9. SECURITY / PRIVACY

Do not inspect or expose email message contents unless a stage explicitly requires it.

Focus on metadata, protocol behavior, TLS, certificates, cryptographic posture, and forensic evidence.

Use safe parsing and input validation for uploaded PCAP/PCAPNG files.

Do not execute arbitrary uploaded content.
